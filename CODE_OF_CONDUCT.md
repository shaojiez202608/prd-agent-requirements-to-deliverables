# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone.

Expected: be kind and constructive, assume good intent, accept feedback on the work, and keep discussion on topic.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language, publishing other people's private information, or
sustained disruption.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.
Report problems to the maintainers through the private contact channel of the hosting platform.
